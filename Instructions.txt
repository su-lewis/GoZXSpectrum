Playable Go (Baduk) for the ZX Spectrum, two-player classic board game. With logic for capturing stones, and end of game scoring (Japanese rules). Recommend speeding up the system, as it takes longer to place down a stone as the game progresses, but only a second or so when sped up.

To play: Just type in your move coordinates. 
To end the game: Both players must pass (p)
